HackerMining

20 years of Hacker News — a data investigation

Hacker News is the tech industry’s water cooler. Since 2006, millions of stories have been submitted, voted on, and discussed. The aggregate of those decisions is a real-time seismograph of what the industry cares about — and when.

This project mines the full public HN dataset (35M+ items via Google BigQuery) to answer questions that are hard to answer from the inside:

  • When exactly did blockchain die on HN?
  • How big was the ChatGPT earthquake — really?
  • What makes a post reach the front page?
  • How unequal is attention on HN?
  • Who actually shapes what the community reads?

The 7 Key Findings

  1. Technology life cycles are shockingly short — most go from hype peak to noise in under 4 years
  2. ChatGPT was a 10× discontinuity, not just a bump — the largest single-event spike in 18 years of data
  3. The best time to post is Tuesday 10am UTC — weekend posts underperform by 30–40%
  4. HN score inequality rivals wealth inequality — Gini > 0.8, top 1% get 40%+ of all points
  5. GitHub replaced personal blogs as the default link target by 2018
  6. AI stories now score 2× the baseline — the topic is an upvote signal independent of quality
  7. ~50 power users drive a disproportionate share of all viral content
